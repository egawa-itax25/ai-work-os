# Portfolio Browser Tab Identity

## Decision

The browser tab for `/portfolio` uses the Japanese title `タスク管理` and a
checklist-style SVG favicon. The in-app navigation remains `ポートフォリオ`.

## Reason

`タスク管理` describes the page's daily purpose at a glance when several
browser tabs are open. A scalable checklist favicon communicates task tracking
without depending on the browser's generic globe fallback.

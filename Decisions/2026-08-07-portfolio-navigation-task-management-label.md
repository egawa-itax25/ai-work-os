# Portfolio Navigation Task Management Label

## Decision

The `/portfolio` navigation item uses the Japanese label `タスク管理` and a
checklist-style inline SVG icon.

## Reason

`タスク管理` describes the daily purpose of the operation hub more directly
than `ポートフォリオ`. A scalable checklist icon stays crisp in the compact
sidebar and communicates task tracking and completion at a glance without
depending on platform-specific symbol rendering.
